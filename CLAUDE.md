# Instructions for AI coding/research agents
This is a research repository, not an authorized software build.

Before substantive work, read CHARTER.md, RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md, and DECISION_LOG.md in full. If absent or still draft, do not treat gate criteria as approved.
RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md is the only source of gate definitions and terms; CHARTER.md sets thesis, scope and authority. Where this file or a prompt conflicts with them, they govern; stop and report the conflict.
Preserve scope, decision thresholds and evidence requirements. No autonomous pivot, no industry preference, no premature compiler/interpreter/agent swarm or full product.
Research may suggest language semantics, but cannot assume that a proprietary language must be built. A language is an optional way to implement a semantic mechanism, not the goal.
Never assert that OpenAI/Anthropic or any vendor can or cannot perform a task without current evidence. Competition is neither an automatic veto nor automatic evidence of opportunity.
Where web/external browsing is unavailable, explicitly report the coverage limitation; do not create fake citations or pretend to have completed the literature scan.
Mark observations, inferences, assumptions and unknowns. Original sources, effective dates, negative evidence and reproducibility take precedence over eloquence.
Use the strongest relevant incumbent solutions as baselines. Distinguish novel syntax, novel semantics, and operational integration.
Do not fabricate results, buyer quotes, source material, or test outcomes. Reference labels derived from the proposed mechanism's own rule model are exploratory only, never conclusive.
Do not modify frozen criteria, the charter, or preregistered experiments without human approval. Propose changes via a separate diff and explain effect on prior decisions.
Never merge, deploy or run paid experiments without explicit authorization.
Only the owner approves (CHARTER.md § Authority). When the owner gives an explicit approval in conversation or on a PR, record it accurately in DECISION_LOG.md (date, where, words or faithful paraphrase, object, version). Never infer approval from silence and never approve your own proposals.
Prefer small files, simple scripts and transparent outputs. Keep source information and provenance with research artifacts.
When finishing a task report files changed, observed outcomes, unresolved gaps, and a suggested GO/HOLD/STOP decision (not a self-approved gate).
