## Essential corrections (must implement)
1. **Prevent circular truth labels.** Predeclare provenance, independent reference answers, holdout isolation, who adjudicates and when. Independent means reference outcomes are justified by external authoritative material or a reviewer/checker insulated from D's implementation; avoid blanket wording that the *same human* can never work on both evaluation and implementation, since the project has a solo founder. Self-generated labels from D's specification are exploratory only, never conclusive. Disclose synthetic-only limitations. Source of truth must not be D's rule model.
2. **Prevent straw-man baselines.** Explicitly define A = frontier LLM with appropriate tools, B = LLM plus robust structured schema/host code, C = strongest relevant existing DSL/rules/constraint/other incumbent solution when such an incumbent materially applies, D = proposed semantic mechanism, possibly hosted in normal code. Define LLM's role per stage, including possibility that it is a *build aid only* rather than runtime component. Comparisons should use comparable data, engineering attention and competence, a shared development set, frozen holdout, transparent deviations and a documented baseline-steelmanning pass. No unrealistic demand for exact token, turn and wall-clock equality. No C arm chosen purely to be easy to beat.
3. **Isolate novelty.** The mechanism added by D must be named; show what B and the best incumbent cannot do *as well*, on specific counterexamples and measurable properties. Differentiate novel semantics, novel syntax, existing libraries and integration. A win over LLM-only A does not establish language necessity. A language is an optional implementation method, not the outcome sought.
4. **One primary endpoint per preregistered experiment.** Choose one of correctness, change/update effort, independent verifiability or lifecycle cost before looking at holdout results. Specify concrete error severity, regression guardrails, generalization limits, evaluation N and uncertainty; require an evaluation that can detect a meaningful difference or label it exploratory. Do not treat the initial 30% / 50% figures as universal hard cutoffs, nor require a predetermined confidence interval across all task types. Freeze the success rule before holdout exposure; distinguish exploratory pilot tuning from confirmatory tests.
5. **G1 is plausibility, NOT logical inexpressibility.** IMPORTANT OVERRIDE of Review 000 amendment RP-05: reject its sentence that baseline tools 'demonstrably cannot express' the construct and its automatic STOP if either ~60-line encoding satisfies three checks. General-purpose languages and existing engines can often encode a behavior but only with brittle per-use reimplementation. The potential advantage is expressiveness with lower implementation/update cost, static checks, consistency, auditability, temporal correctness, safer composition or other *measurable* domain value. Use a light paper expressibility/maintenance probe at G1; hard proof only at G3.
6. **Commercial evidence may precede G3.** IMPORTANT OVERRIDE of proposed RP-24: early customer pain, workarounds, incumbent pricing and buyer reachability should inform G0/ranking; a technical experiment must not manufacture market validation. After G3 technical success, deeper buyer outreach can inform commercial G4. Do not force exactly 3 post-G3 interviews or automatic SHELVE on non-response. Separate a technical result, a commercial hypothesis, and actual validation.
7. **Lightweight approvals and HOLD.** Only owner can approve charter freeze, shortlist, experiments, extensions and pivots. Clear explicit approval in conversation/PR is valid authority if the agent records it accurately in DECISION_LOG.md; do NOT demand that the mobile owner personally commit a log entry. No approval by silence; no self-approval by Claude. Every HOLD names missing evidence and one bounded next action; unresolved items are PARKED at cycle-end rather than silently failed or held forever.
8. **Bounded program with no invented budget.** Retain a single initial search cycle, target 20–30 micro-workflows across >=6 industries (report fewer when evidence is lacking); up to 10 detailed cards, shortlist preferably 3 plus up to 2 reserves; at most 2 experiments and explicit owner-approved expansion. Keep an explicit time/spend/owner-hours cap field, but do not fill numbers without owner authorization. The initial research must not be blocked by arbitrary baseline-equality or gold-label rules that cannot be tested until a candidate is selected; flag G2 as provisional at discovery until its oracle is substantiated.
9. **No inherited industry advantage, no hypothetical future-lab veto.** Competitors validate potential demand but are robust baselines, not automatic rejection or automatic opportunity. Reframe the 'frontier AI improved' stress test to evaluate concrete vulnerabilities to improvement, not speculative future capability as established fact.
10. **Traceability.** Single canonical location for gates and definitions (RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md); CHARTER sets thesis and boundaries; prompts must defer to them. Append a DRAFT revision proposal in DECISION_LOG without rewriting its original entry. Keep candidate cards readable, sources cited, and proposals separate from owner decisions.
