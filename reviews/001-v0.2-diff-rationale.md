## (a) Changes adopted

| Task 001 correction | What v0.2 does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1. No circular truth labels | Reference outcomes are classed E (external authoritative), I (insulated reviewer or separate session that has not seen D's rule model; the solo founder qualifies if labels are fixed from sources before D's rule model exists) or X (derived from D: exploratory only). Confirmatory holdouts need E or I. Disputes are resolved from authoritative material, never from D's rule model. Synthetic generators may not derive from D; synthetic-only results are disclosed as limited. Public worked examples get perturbed or post-cutoff variants where available. | PROTOCOL § 1, § 6.1–6.2; CHARTER principles; CLAUDE.md |
| 2. No straw-man baselines | Arms A–D defined once. C is the strongest incumbent that materially applies (DSL, rules, constraint, workflow or other), chosen by fit and never for being easy to beat; if none applies, the omission is recorded and claims are limited. The LLM role is stated per stage, including build aid only. Shared development set, frozen holdout, comparable attention and competence, a documented baseline-steelmanning pass, and logged deviations. No exact token, turn or clock equality. | PROTOCOL § 1, § 5, § 6.3–6.4 |
| 3. Isolate novelty | M is named; D without M approximates B and is run as an ablation; the four kinds of difference (novel semantics, novel syntax, existing library, integration) are defined. A win over A alone does not establish need. A G3 GO validates the mechanism, not a language. | PROTOCOL § 1, § 6.5, § 7; CHARTER mission |
| 4. One primary endpoint | Exactly one of correctness, change/update effort, independent verifiability or lifecycle cost per experiment. Severity scale, guardrails, generalization limits, N, an uncertainty method suited to the task, and the smallest meaningful difference; otherwise the run is exploratory. The success rule is frozen before holdout exposure and justified by operator value, not by D's dev results. 30% / 50% are illustrative only. No mandated interval level. | CHARTER hypothesis; PROTOCOL § 6.6–6.7 |
| 5. G1 is plausibility | Light paper probe on 2–3 counterexamples. STOP only if the strongest alternative offers the property as a documented feature at comparable cost, or the advantage is syntax only. Encodability alone is not STOP. | PROTOCOL § 3 |
| 6. Commercial evidence may precede G3 | Demand signals inform G0 and ranking. Three labels are kept separate: demand signal, commercial hypothesis, validation (actual commitment). No interview quota and no automatic shelving. | CHARTER payoffs; PROTOCOL § 1, § 4, § 8 |
| 7. Light approvals and HOLD | Owner-only decisions are listed once. An explicit approval in conversation or on a PR is valid when the agent records it accurately in DECISION_LOG.md; the owner need not commit it. No approval by silence and no self-approval. Every HOLD names missing evidence and one bounded next action; unresolved HOLDs become PARKED at cycle end. | CHARTER § Authority; PROTOCOL § 9; CLAUDE.md |
| 8. Bounded program, no invented budget | 20–30 candidates, at least 6 industries, up to 10 cards, preferably 3 finalists plus up to 2 reserves, at most 2 experiments; expansion only by owner approval. Cap fields exist but are left unset. G2 is provisional at discovery, and experiment-fairness rules do not apply at discovery. | CHARTER § Scope and timebox; PROTOCOL § 3 |
| 9. No inherited industry, no future-lab veto | Sector-neutral principle; outcome-shaped discovery channels; coverage report by invariant type. Competitors indicate demand and set the baseline, neither veto nor proof. The stress test now separates structural advantages from those resting on current model error rates and asks only for concrete, dated vulnerabilities. | CHARTER principles; PROTOCOL § 2; prompt 01 |
| 10. Traceability | RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md is the only home of gates and definitions; CHARTER sets thesis, scope, caps and authority; prompts, template and CLAUDE.md defer and must report conflicts. A Proposed v0.2 entry is appended to DECISION_LOG.md. Template gate lines are marked as agent proposals. | All files |

## (c) Open owner choices

- **Budget numbers:** calendar per cycle, owner hours per week, spend per discovery run and per experiment. They are left unset, and agents will not fill them.
- **Scoring:** v0.2 keeps the 20/20/15/15/10/10/10 weights and adds short glosses. The owner may change weights, for example founder-fit or competitive opening, before discovery.
- **Initial research tool for prompt 01:** Claude Code with web access, a hosted deep-research product, or a human-assisted session. This affects whether links can be verified and whether the prompt must be fully self-contained.
- **LLM runtime scope:** whether an LLM must be part of any resulting artifact at runtime, or is a build aid only. Arms and roles accommodate both; the choice changes which comparator matters most.
- **Non-blocking:** the GitHub repository description; whether the finalist count stays at 3 plus 2 reserves.
